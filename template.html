Dashed: mean of the full weeks. Band: usual spread (largest deviation of a full week from the mean). Faded bars: week only partly counted. ⚑ = weekly limit hit.

The API-price equivalent weighs the models by their list prices. How the limit weighs them is unknown: if the mix shifts, the dollar value of the quota can change although Anthropic changed nothing.

Index: full weeks average 100. With a constant quota and fully used weeks, the measure that best reflects the limit stays flat. The trend line is a linear regression over the full weeks. Hollow and dotted: the current week, not yet fully counted; it is not part of band and trend, the trend is only extended to it.

Cost: equivalent at API list prices, with the prices in prices.json that were valid in each hour. In small print: change against the week before. Quota: up to the first weekly limit hit; ≥ at least (full week without a limit hit).
